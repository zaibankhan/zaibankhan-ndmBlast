NDM Variants Information

What are NDM variants?

New Delhi Metallo-β-lactamase (NDM) enzymes are carbapenemases that confer resistance to almost all β-lactam antibiotics. There are many NDM variants (NDM-1 through NDM-40+), each differing by small changes in their amino-acid (protein) and hence nucleotide (DNA) sequence. Identifying the exact variant is important for epidemiology and clinical management.

Using this site

  • Use BLASTn to compare a DNA sequence against the NDM nucleotide database.
  • Use BLASTp to compare a protein sequence against the NDM protein database.
  • View the top matches, then click a variant for its full metadata, the pairwise alignment, and links to AlphaFold / PDB / NCBI / publications.
